# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation (current)

1. Backend skeleton: Fastify, config, logging, errors, database, Redis, queues, security, health, OpenAPI.
2. Auth: phone OTP with test numbers, JWT access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, web cookies and mobile Bearer.
3. IAM: users, workspaces, members, `/me`.
4. Meta connection: requirements, token validation, encryption, asset discovery, primary asset selection.
5. Onboarding state machine: steps, status, next step.

## Phase 1 — MVP (Arogya System as first customer)

6. Business profile: website analysis with Claude, editable profile.
7. Goal and budget: goal, monthly budget, hard spending cap, recommended daily budget.
8. Tracking check: dataset activity, Conversions API, domain verification, conversion event choice.
9. Campaign plan: AI plan with phases, audiences, budget split; approval.
10. Creatives: copy and image generation, video uploads, policy check.
11. Review and launch: previews, paused creation in Meta, activation on approval with idempotency.
12. Autopilot v1: insights sync, daily briefing, approval inbox, automatic pause of losers within guardrails, audit log.

## Phase 2 — Growth

13. Organic publishing to the Page and Instagram, content calendar.
14. Comment and message replies with approval rules.
15. Lead form sync, notifications by email and WhatsApp.
16. Mobile app on the same API.

## Phase 3 — Market

17. Meta App Review and Business verification for multi-customer access, Facebook Login for Business.
18. Agency mode: multiple client workspaces, white-label reports.
19. Billing and plans.
20. Google Ads.
